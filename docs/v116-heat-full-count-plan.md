# Full-count heat stress fixture — plan

This is the proposed replacement for the failed partial-fluid fixture. It is a fixture plan, not a measured result; all layout and activity assertions must pass before any latency number is accepted.

## Hot working/cooling fixture

- Place 25 standard 2×2 reactor modules in a 5×5 grid, with enough spacing to keep their fluid networks separate. Each module starts from the shipped 48-HX/84-turbine blueprint. Remove eight HX and four turbines during setup, leaving exactly 40 HX and 80 turbines per module. Prefer removing two HX from each of the module's four real water networks, and remove turbine devices only after their actual steam-network assignment is known. Assert final totals: 100 reactors, 1,000 HX, and 2,000 turbines.
- Keep the connected 100×100 heat-pipe core (10,000 real pipes and its 19,800 internal edges). The 25 modules retain their own connected reactor/heat-pipe/HX assemblies; count and report every station pipe and edge as well as the core. Do not label the module components as one graph unless the graph actually connects them.
- Each 40-HX module has four water manifolds with ten HX each. Install two real offshore pumps at each manifold's inlet (eight per module, 200 total). Only the fixture terrain may be prepared as shallow water before simulation starts; after the first `Game.step`, pumps, fluid networks, heat exchangers, steam lines, and turbines must change through normal simulation only. Assert all 100 water networks receive water, all 1,000 HX have a real water and steam port, and each HX records at least one working tick.
- Preserve the blueprint's steam piping and turbine ports. Assert every turbine has a non-conflicting steam network with positive steam during the run, each engine's `_out` becomes positive under load, and the aggregate real turbine output serves 100 powered standard radars (30 MW). Distribute radar loads so every independent turbine power network has actual demand; do not hand-edit fluid or power counters.
- Before the first operating tick, seed the same explicit 550°C thermal fixture in every heat node, provide one real fuel cell to each reactor, and enable normal reactor operation. (550°C is above the exchangers' strict 500°C operating threshold while remaining below the 900°C warning threshold.) Thereafter, use `Game.step` for the hot-working and cooling stages. Keep `noBoom=false`; emergency-stop every reactor through the normal command path, assert all rods stop, no accident or radiation zone appears during cooling, and report residual temperatures and energy ledgers.
- Exercise a genuine split/merge on the 100×100 core through normal entity removal/build and graph invalidation. Separately run the actual 2×2 station accident with a seeded 1,100°C thermal state, accidents enabled, and verify the real 600-hot-tick threshold and radiation zone.
- Gate each captured stage at full-tick p95 ≤16.67 ms and heat-profiler p95 ≤6 ms (raised from 2 ms by owner decision 2026-10-12, allowed 6–10 ms). Keep steady hot/cooling/cold profiles, split/merge rebuild samples, and the accident window separately reported so an expensive event cannot disappear inside a pooled percentile.

## Cold isolated fixture and timing evidence

- Retain exactly 100 reactors, 10,000 core-grid pipes plus 3,096 counted extension pipes, 1,000 HX, and 2,000 turbines. Place the 10,000 grid pipes at two-tile spacing in a 100×100 block; arrange the 3,096 extension pipes as 56×55 and 16 short-row blocks; arrange reactors 20×5, HX 40×25, and turbines 40×50 in separate compact blocks with at least one open tile between heat ports. Assert 14,196 thermal nodes, zero heat edges, and exactly 15°C for every node.
- Size `flatten` to the actual fixture bounds plus a small margin. The current cold layout uses half-open bounds `[-10,670) × [-10,660)`, covering 22×22 = 484 32-tile chunks, rather than an elongated single row. Record world bounds, generated chunk count, and setup/build seconds separately for hot and cold worlds; they are setup diagnostics, not timed `Game.step` samples.
- Persist the report incrementally after preflight and after every measured stage. Include status (`in-progress`, `failed`, or `passed`), completed stage samples/quantiles, actual counts, and `acceptanceFailures`. An assertion or uncaught error must synchronously write the partial report and retain exit code 1. This prevents a failed cooling/accident gate from discarding already measured p95 values.
- The final report must distinguish per-step full-tick p95 from the heat profiler p95 and list the actual engine CPU/runtime, warmup/sample counts, hot/cold world bounds, setup time, pump-fed HX count, steam-fed turbine count, actual load/output, accidents, and every failed acceptance assertion.
